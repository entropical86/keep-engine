Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What Keep Engine stores

All notes and settings are stored locally on your own computer. Keep Engine has no server and does not send your notes anywhere.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Keep Engine uses Google's API to read and create events on the calendars you select in its settings. This data travels directly between your computer and Google. It is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Your sign-in token is stored locally on your computer. You can disconnect at any time in Keep Engine's settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Keep Engine's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is not collected

No analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no data is sold or shared.
